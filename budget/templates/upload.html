Getting a CSV from US Bank

  1. Sign in at usbank.com and open the checking account or card.
  2. On the transactions list, choose the download or export option.
  3. Pick a date range and the CSV format.
  4. Upload the file here and choose the matching account.

Files from other banks work too, as long as they have a date column, a description or name column, and an amount (or debit and credit) column.
