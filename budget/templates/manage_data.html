Erase everything

Starts the app over as if just installed. Use it to hand the app to someone else, or to start from scratch.

  • Erased: all {{ '{:,}'.format(counts.transactions) }} transactions, {{ '{:,}'.format(counts.accounts) }} accounts and their balances, categories and the merchant dictionary you built, the plan, recurring and tax notes, Amazon items, the bankroll, bank sync account links, and every setting (notifications included).
  • Stays: the files in the data folder: uploaded bank files and workbooks, personal.toml, the bank sync and email passwords, and these restore points. The built-in categories and dictionary come back. Bank sync stays connected, but its accounts need linking again.
  • Backups: a restore point is saved first, and older copies stay in your backups. The daily backup knows this erase was meant, so it backs up the empty app as the newest copy without touching older ones.
