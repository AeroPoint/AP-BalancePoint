Accounts

Every account you track, open or closed. Closing an account keeps its transactions and balance history in every report; it just stops asking for new balances after the month it closed. If one account shows up under two names, merge them. “Bank exports from” is the month of the last balance you checked against the bank: months before it stay as recorded, and uploaded bank exports are the record from its 1st on.

Loan schedules

A loan with fixed terms doesn't need monthly balances: what's owed is worked out from the amount borrowed, the rate, the length and the start date. “Counts from” is when the calculation takes over; months before it keep the balances already recorded. Extra principal payments aren't included, so adjust the terms if you make them.

Paycheck retirement savings

A 401(k) comes out of a paycheck before it reaches the bank, so no export shows it. Enter it here and it counts as saving on the Overview and in savings rates. Each entry applies to paychecks from its start date until the next entry with the same paycheck text, so for a raise or a new rate, add an entry instead of editing the old one. The match is the employer's % of your contribution, on contributions up to the cap (75% up to 8% means 8% of yours earns 6%).

Business accounts

Give an account its own categories and everything on it lands there: money in to its income, money out to its expenses, whatever the merchant. Transfers to and from your other accounts stay Transfers, and a category you pick on a single transaction (a personal charge made on the business card) always stays.
